About

How much of a track record can a follower actually earn? That question runs through everything I build.

I learned the problem with my own money: I copied leaders from the top of a leaderboard and lost. The records were real; the inference was false. Since then I have priced the gap (hindsight, attrition and copy costs) and built Talyxion to act on it. Finding an edge is easy; proving it is not an artifact is the work, so the most valuable code I write is the gate that says no. I am a PhD student in Data Science & Information Technology at Tsinghua University (Shenzhen), where the same question carries into disaster response: how much of what a model shows holds up in the real world.

Track-record audits

47,220 leaderboard wallets: Sharpe 3.4 with hindsight vs 1.3 point-in-time. 1 in 3 accounts stop within a year. Copying at the leader's hour costs ~9.5 bp a trade vs ~3.5 bp four hours later.

ML & optimization

Multi-modal & multi-agent ML, regime detection, evolutionary alpha discovery, hierarchical RL market making.

OR for impact

Stochastic optimization for post-disaster response — earthquake EMS resource allocation and tropical-cyclone forecasting.
